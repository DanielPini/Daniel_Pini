Conducting

Woollahra Philharmonic in Sydney (2024 and 2026), 900 Ensemble in Switzerland, and the Worthing Philharmonic, The Film Orchestra and Locus Ensemble in England.

Assistant conductor of The People's Orchestra and The Rusty Orchestra (2019 to 2022), and the English premiere of Caerwen Martin's Stars Come Out in a Midnight Sky. Assistant to David Curtis, mentored by Alexander Briger, and trained in contemporary repertoire with Arturo Tamayo.

Certificate in Advanced Conducting, Conservatorio della Svizzera italiana (2025). EAMA / Cynthia Lilley Fellowship (2023). Arts Council England DYCP grant (2022).

Cello

Guest cellist with the Philharmonia Orchestra, Opera Australia Orchestra, and the Sydney and Tasmanian Symphony Orchestras.

Southbank Sinfonia (2019), Sydney Symphony Fellow (2018) and Junge Deutsche Oper (2015 to 2016). Solo recitals at Festival Dino Ciani and the Adelaide Cello Festival, and in 2026 a masterclass at the Riverina Conservatorium of Music in Wagga Wagga.

BMus, Sydney Conservatorium of Music. MMus, Musikhochschule Stuttgart.

BEING

Solo cello works by five living Australian composers: Pham, Dean, Cheetham Fraillon, Lim and Vine.

Released by Move Records in 2024, with support from Creative Australia. Reviewed in Limelight.

FABLE ARTS

An arts organisation that curated concerts, exhibitions and events across music, visual art, writing and science, and paid every artist it worked with.

I co-founded it with Victoria Pham in 2020. We directed it together until 2023, and I directed it on my own from then until 2026. Projects included The Parts We Give, a video game for Christine Pan's song cycle, and Navigating Distance and Closeness with Niki Johnson and Hamish Upton, whose ceramic instruments you can play online.
